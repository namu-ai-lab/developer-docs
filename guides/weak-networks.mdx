Once awake, the Namu API answers in seconds. The slow part is usually the last kilometre: the phone's connection. These habits make the difference between an app people use and one they give up on.

## Send and receive small audio

- **Ask for `opus`** from `/v1/speech`: about 40 KB for ten seconds, against 480 KB as WAV. Use `mp3` where Opus does not play.
- **Upload voice notes as they are.** A WhatsApp OGG is already small; converting it to WAV first makes it about ten times larger.
- **Record in mono**, at the phone's normal speech settings. Stereo and high sample rates add bytes, not accuracy.

## Make fewer round trips

- **Speech in, speech out in one call.** `/v1/translate` with `include_audio` returns the translation and its audio together.
- **Generate shared audio once.** A daily announcement played to thousands of people should be one `/v1/speech` call, stored on your server or a CDN, not one call per listener.
- **Read `/v1/voices` once** when your app starts, not before each request.

## Handle the line dropping

- **Keep the recording until the answer arrives.** If the upload fails, retry from the saved file instead of asking the person to speak again.
- **Retry with a pause.** On a network error, `502` or `504`, wait a second, then two, then four, and stop after a few tries. A request that fails counts nothing against your allowance.
- **Use a generous timeout.** The first request after a quiet spell can take a minute or more while the model wakes up. Set timeouts in minutes, not seconds, and show the person that something is happening.

```python Python
import time
import requests

def post_with_retries(url, tries=4, **kwargs):
    for attempt in range(tries):
        try:
            response = requests.post(url, timeout=300, **kwargs)
            if response.status_code not in (429, 502, 503, 504):
                return response
            wait = int(response.headers.get("Retry-After", 2 ** attempt))
        except requests.ConnectionError:
            wait = 2 ** attempt
        time.sleep(wait)
    return response
```

<Note>
  Do not retry `429 quota_exceeded`: the month's allowance is used, and only next month (or a higher allowance) changes that.
</Note>

## Show progress people can trust

Speech takes a moment to make and to download. Say so: a short sound or a "listening…" state when recording ends, and play the answer as soon as the file arrives. On slow lines, a text version of the answer shown first (the `text`, `reply` or `source_text` field) lets people start reading while the audio loads.

## Work offline where you can

Queue recordings when there is no connection and send them when it returns; store audio you generated so a second listen costs nothing. Voice notes in the Sahel often travel when the network allows, not when they are recorded, and your app can do the same.
